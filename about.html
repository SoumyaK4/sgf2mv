About

SGF to Music Video turns Go games into a nice music videos.

Upload an SGF file and the app parses the move sequence, animates it on a wooden board, and maps each move into deterministic notes. The same game always produces the same musical shape, while controls for scale, chords, instrument, register, pacing, and board texture let each render take on a different character.

You can find more Go projects at SoumyaK4.in.

How to use the simple mode

Open the Simple page, choose an SGF file, then press Play to hear the game locally. Each new stone and its surrounding intersections choose notes from D, Eb, G, A, and Bb, while growing board density gradually lifts the pitch. Use Playback for pace, volume, legato, and drone, Music for instrument and register, and Visuals & Export for board style and video size. Use Render video to export a WebM.

How to use complex mode

Open the Complex page when you want different parts of the board to sound different. Board Parts lets you enable corners, sides, or centre, then assign melodic instruments and musical settings to that area. Flow & Texture controls legato, dovetailing, voice leading, glissando, doubling, homophony, through-composed flow, transitions, and organic unity. Neighborhood notes can be enabled globally or inside individual board parts. Move ranges can add extra instruments, including percussion, for specific phases of the game. Sustained Layers add sounds over the move notes for the whole game or a move range, and the Every field controls whether that layer plays every move, every second move, every third move, and so on.
